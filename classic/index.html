A research project: an online-learning hybrid ensemble — random forests, gradient boosting, LSTM, Transformer, LEAR, merit-order and Chronos members, combined per hour by Bernstein online aggregation, with QRA + adaptive-conformal quantiles — retrained every day on a strict 07:30 UTC D−1 information set and published before gate closure. Forecast errors are published; realised auction prices are shown under their source licences — historical days from the Ember European wholesale price dataset (CC-BY-4.0), the newest days from Nord Pool N2EX (rolling 60-day window).
